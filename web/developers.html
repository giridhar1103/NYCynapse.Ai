Connect your app.

NYCynapse is also an MCP server. Add it to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code or anything that speaks the Model Context Protocol, and your own model can look up New York as it is right now. Every tool is read-only.

1. Get access

  1. Ask. Send your name, email and what you will use it for on the request page. You get a private status link. Save it.
  2. Wait for review. Requests are read by a person. Nothing is emailed; the status link shows the answer.
  3. Copy your address. Once approved, the status link shows your personal server address, one time. Treat it like a password.

2. Add it to your app

Paste it to fill in the steps below. It stays on this page and is not sent or saved.

3. Ask something

Good first questions once it is connected:

  • When is the next L at Bedford Av?
  • Step-free way from Penn Station to the Met tomorrow at 10?
  • Anything going on near Union Square?
  • Is the G running normally this weekend?

Limits and safety

  • Usage limits apply during the beta, by the hour and by the day. Yours are on your status page. A call past the limit returns an error that says so.
  • Every tool is read-only. There is nothing to send, buy, change or delete.
  • Results carry a short summary, the data, and the sources with the time of the reading. Feeds lag and plans change, so show that time to your readers.
  • Times are New York time.
  • Anyone who has your address uses your limits. If it leaks, send a new request and say so.

If it does not work

401, unknown or revoked token
The address is incomplete or no longer active. Copy all of it, including the part after /mcp/.
The app asks you to sign in
There is no sign-in. The address is the key. Choose no authentication, or leave those fields empty.
The app only runs local servers
Use the Other tab. mcp-remote runs on your machine and passes calls on.
Connected, but the model does not use it
Turn the connector on in that chat, or say "use NYCynapse".
